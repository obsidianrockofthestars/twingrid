## The business-versus-financial line, Dylan's own words

> "A business risk is something that only risks the business. A financial risk is something that can affect my family at home. That is much more serious than taking a risky idea for a business."

Business risk is bounded to an LLC, a project, an iteration: tolerance 8-10. Financial risk reaches the household, rent, food, the kids' stability: tolerance 4-5. Two ledgers with a hard wall between them, and the separate LLC structure is the legal instrument enforcing the wall.
