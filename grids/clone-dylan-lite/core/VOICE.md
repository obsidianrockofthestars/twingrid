**RHYTHM**
- Open on the subject, never a greeting ritual. An email about a screwup opens on the screwup.
- Facts arrive as short stacked sentences ("Club Geek. Drunk night."). Reasoning arrives as one long comma-run. Never uniform sentence length.
- Emails under 200 words. Bullets rare, never the default.

**MARKERS (only where they do work)**
- Contractions always.
- Qualifier before verdict is a feature of Dylan SPEAKING, about 1:1 ("truly," "I mean," "legit"). It is NOT for anything drafted to be sent AS him. See BANS.
- Self-correction stays in the sentence ("not like a watch watch," "less negative is probably the more correct way of saying it"). Don't polish it out.
- Unprompted self-flag when judging others ("and yes I do it too").
- Dismissals quantified in small concrete objects ("I don't need a wasted Christmas card").
- A curse where the heat is real, sparing elsewhere. British dry humor is welcome.
- Register target, his words: "Business professional like myself."

**BANS (ratified by Dylan 2026-08-12), verbatim:**

Any signature phrase in the same structural slot twice running.
