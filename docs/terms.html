1.2 What the service is

Personakind is a tool for writing "personas": grids of plain-text cells that describe how an AI should think and talk. You can keep a persona private, publish it on a public page, chat with it using your own API key, or chat with it through our hosted chat by buying messages. Personas can carry an image and a voice you choose. The service is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, to the extent the law allows.

Private personas are shown only to you and to people you have explicitly allowed to operate your account. You are responsible for what your personas say and contain. Do not publish personas that impersonate a real person without that person's consent, that contain someone else's private information, that are unlawful, or that are hate speech. We may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules, and readers can report any public persona.

1.4 AI output

Replies from a persona are generated by an AI model from the cells you or another author wrote. They can be wrong, made up, or unlike what the author intended. They are not advice of any kind. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions. Every public persona page carries an AI disclosure, and you must not remove it.

1.5 Paid messages, credits and limits

Hosted chat is sold as a pack of messages ("credits") on a monthly or yearly plan through RevenueCat and Stripe. One reply normally costs one message; a very large persona costs two or three and the chat drawer says so before you send. A generated persona image costs 15 messages. Hearing a reply in a Gemini voice costs 2 to 6 messages depending on length. Free features (chat on your own key, image upload, the browser voice) cost nothing. Limits, stated so nobody is surprised: 10 generated images and 200 voiced replies per day per account; unused messages expire when the plan period ends and renewal resets the balance to the pack; when the site reaches its monthly capacity, hosted chat and image creation pause and your messages stay yours until they expire. A failed call is refunded automatically. Payments are handled by Stripe under RevenueCat's checkout; we never see or store your card number. Plans renew until cancelled and you can cancel any time from the receipt email or by contacting us; refunds for time already used are at our discretion, and refunds required by law are always given.

1.6 Your own API key

3. Security and what happens if there is a breach

If we learn of unauthorised access to personal data we hold, we will: contain it; notify every affected person by the email on their account without unreasonable delay and within the time the law requires (Missouri law, RSMo 407.1500, and the law of the state or country where you live); say what was taken, when, and what we did about it; and notify the authorities where the law requires.
