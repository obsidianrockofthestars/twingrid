What a persona is

Not a black box. A page you can read.

Most AI "personalities" are a mystery box: you type things in and hope. Yours is a page of short, labelled notes: who you are, what to do, what never to do, how you sound. Anyone can read it, including you. If a line is wrong, you change the line. What you read is exactly what it is.

Want more? There is an advanced editor for people who like knobs. You never have to open it.

Want Claude to load personas by itself? Add the Personakind connector: in Claude go to Customize, Connectors, Add custom connector, and paste https://personakind.com/mcp. Hosted chat

Hosted chat: $5 a month, or $48 a year

  • No API key, nothing to set up. Talk to any public persona, or your own.
  • Runs on Claude Haiku: fast, and cheap on purpose.
  • Hard cap: 500 messages a month, or 6,000 a year to use whenever you like. Unused messages stay until the period ends. Cancel any time.
  • The limits, so nobody is surprised: a reply is 1 message (a very large persona is 2 or 3, the drawer says so); a persona image is 15 messages, up to 10 a day; hearing a reply costs 2 to 6, up to 200 a day. When the site is near its monthly capacity, hosted chat pauses and your messages stay yours.
  • Rather use your own key? That stays free, always.

No key? Signed-in readers can use hosted chat: 500 messages a month for $5, nothing to set up.

Or bring your own key. It stays in this browser only (localStorage) and is sent straight to Anthropic, never to us. The current composition becomes the clone's system prompt.

No key yet? Get one at console.anthropic.com. Prefer not to paste a key in a browser? Use the local runner tab.
