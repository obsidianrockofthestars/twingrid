Welcome to TwinGrid 👋

You're about to build an AI version of you that you can actually read and correct — not a black box. Here's the 30-second version.

What you can do

  • Pick a template (Creator, Coach, Founder…) and make it yours — or start from blank.
  • Fill 19 facets of small cells: who you are, what you do, what you never do, how you sound.
  • Flip modes — Single, Multi (all specialists at once), or Queue (a council) — and chat with your twin.
  • Make a twin public and get a shareable profile page in its own color.

What you get, free

  • 8 starter templates (more on the way) + the full node Studio to edit every cell.
  • Sign in with Google, password, or a magic link.
  • A public profile page for any twin.
  • Export your grid and run the open-source engine yourself — you own it.

Let an AI fill it for you (MCP)

  • Quick way: hit “Get AI help” and paste the prompt into any Claude or ChatGPT chat — it interviews you.
  • Power way (MCP): download the TwinGrid MCP, pip install -e ., add it to Claude Desktop, then say “interview me and build my TwinGrid.”
