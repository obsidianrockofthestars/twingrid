About

Personakind is a version of you that you can actually read.

An AI persona here is not a black box. It is a grid of short plain-text cells, eighty of them across nineteen facets, that say what the persona knows, what it does, what it never does, the checks it runs, and how it talks. Anyone can read every cell of a public persona before they talk to it. The owner can change any line. That is the whole idea, and as far as we can find, nobody else lets a visitor read the instructions.

Who runs it

Potions and Familiars LLC, a Missouri limited liability company in St. Joseph, Missouri. It is run by one person, Dylan Leeson, who builds with an AI coding partner and stays in the loop on every change. The company also makes Downtown Vibes, a local discovery app for the same town.

Built in the open

The framework under Personakind, TwinGrid, is MIT licensed. The engine that composes a grid into one prompt, the rule checker, the MCP connector and the site itself are in the repo: github.com/obsidianrockofthestars/twingrid. You can export your grid and run it on your own computer with your own key, and nothing touches the web.

Where the AI is

Hosted chat runs on Claude Haiku (Anthropic). Persona portraits and read-aloud voices run on Google models. Your own key runs on whatever you bring. Every published page says its persona is AI, not a person.
