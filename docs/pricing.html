Pricing

Reading any public persona is free, always. Talking to one costs messages, and there are two ways to pay for them: bring your own API key (free from us, you pay your provider directly), or buy a hosted pack and we run the chat for you.
Free, always. Read every cell of every public persona. Build and edit your own. Export your grid as a file and run it on your own computer with the runner in the repo. Bring your own Anthropic key and chat with no limit from us; the key stays in your browser and goes straight to Anthropic.

Hosted chat packs

No key, nothing to set up. Hosted chat runs on Claude Haiku. Buy from the chat drawer on any persona page after you sign in. A pack is a hard cap, not a meter: unused messages stay until the period ends, and a new purchase starts a fresh period at the pack size.

Billing, cancelling, refunds

Payments are handled by Stripe under RevenueCat's checkout; we never see or store your card number. Plans renew until cancelled. Cancel, change plan, update your card or download receipts and invoices from Manage subscription on your Account page or in the receipt email. Changing to another plan happens there, never by buying twice. Refunds for time already used are at our discretion, and refunds required by law are always given; email dylanleeson@potionsandfamiliars.com. The full wording is in the Terms, section 1.5.
