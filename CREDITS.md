# Credits

TwinGrid stands on ideas and teaching from others. Named here on purpose.

## Igor Costa, EvoGraph

The engine's direction is shaped by EvoGraph: Hybrid Directed Graph Evolution toward Software 3.0 (Igor Costa and co-author, arXiv:2508.05199). The idea that an identity, or any system, is best held as a typed graph of small pieces that can be validated, composed, and evolved under measurable pressure is the north star this engine builds toward. Any errors in how that idea is applied here are ours, not the paper's.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05199

## Jeremy Rule, and the Chief AI Officer course

The discipline baked into this engine, human in the loop as a first-class rule, provenance on every claim, refusing to ship an identity that cannot be verified, comes out of the CAIO coursework taught by Jeremy Rule. The engine does not merely allow a human to review; it structurally refuses to compose a broken or drifted identity, which is that teaching turned into code.

## The method

The grid decomposition, the four stacking axes, and the composition receipt were developed by Dylan Leeson while building a working clone of himself, then generalized into this framework so anyone can build their own.
